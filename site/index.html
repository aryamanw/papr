Make every web page read like paper.

Papr is a Chrome extension that lays a fine matte grain over the pages you read. Text stays sharp, and the flat glare of a white screen goes quiet. Try it on this page first.

Texture

How it works

Papr draws one transparent layer over the whole page: a sheet of fine noise, the same kind of irregular grain you see on uncoated paper. It is blended into whatever sits underneath, so the texture settles into white backgrounds and fades out of dark text instead of covering it.

The layer never takes a click, a scroll or a text selection. Pages behave exactly as they did before. On dark pages, Papr switches how the grain blends so it lightens the background slightly instead of muddying it.

What you can change

Texture
Six papers: Print Paper, Woven Fabric, Fine Press, Aged Newsprint, Torinoko Washi and Parchment. Newsprint and Parchment also add a little warmth.
Intensity
From a barely-there 15% up to 50%. Most people settle around 20–25%.
On or off
One switch in the toolbar popup. Papr starts switched off, so nothing changes until you turn it on.

Your settings sync through your Chrome profile, so they follow you to other computers.

Install

Papr isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so you load it yourself. It takes about a minute.

  1. Download papr-1.2.5.zip from the latest release.
  2. Unzip it to a folder you'll keep, since Chrome loads the extension from there.
  3. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome.
  4. Turn on Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  5. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.

Pin Papr from the puzzle-piece menu so the switch is always one click away.

What Papr can access

Chrome will ask you to allow Papr on all sites, because it needs to draw the texture on every page you open. Papr doesn't read page content, and it makes no network requests at all. Its settings pages are locked down so that they can't send any.

It also uses Chrome's storage to remember your settings, and the tabs permission to update the texture on open tabs when you change something.
